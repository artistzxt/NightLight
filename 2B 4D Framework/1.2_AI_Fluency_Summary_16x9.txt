The Al Fluency 
Framework 
Four interconnected competencies necessary to ensure our interactions with 
AI are effective, efficient, ethical and safe. 
The 4D Framework 
Delegation 
Discernment 
Key Concepts 
Setting goals and deciding whether, when and 
how to engage with Al. 
Accurately assessing the usefulness of 
Al outputs and behaviours. 
Three Modes of AI interaction: 
• Automation: AI executes specific tasks based on human instructions 
• Augmentation: Humans and AI collaborate as thinking partners 
• Agency: Humans configure AI to independently perform future tasks 
on their behalf
