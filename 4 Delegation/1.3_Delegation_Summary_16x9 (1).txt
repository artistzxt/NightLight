The4Ds: 
Delegation 
Delegation is making thoughtful decisions about what work is appropriate for 
you to do, for AI to do, or for you and AI to do together, and how to distribute 
those tasks. 
~ 
What am I trying to do? 
Problem Awareness 
Clearly understanding your goals and the nature 
of the work before involving AI. 
~ 
What do you do well? 
Platform Awareness 
Understanding the capabilities and limitations 
of different AI systems. 
Effective delegation requires both domain expertise and an understanding 
of AI capabilities.
